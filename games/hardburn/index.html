MK-IV UNGUIDED — OPERATOR NOTES

The motor runs while you hold it

SPACE launches you off the rail, and after that the motor burns for exactly as long as you keep holding it. Let go and you are a dart: gravity takes over, drag bleeds you, and the only thing keeping you up is the speed you already bought. The clock does not start until you light it.

Aim, nose, and where you actually go

Three different directions, and the gap between them is the game. The mouse sets your aim — screen-relative, no roll to untangle. WASD swings the airframe off that line, and thrust follows the airframe, so you can burn sideways out of a corner or swing right round and retro-burn while still looking where you are going. The circle on the HUD is your real flight path. It goes amber when it stops agreeing with your aim.

Banking

Only the improvement over your personal best on a course banks at full rate. Re-flying a course you have already beaten pays a fraction. Grinding is not a strategy — flying better is.

Ordnance

Crates issue airframes. Common frames are paint. From RARE up the airframe geometry itself changes — length, fins, mass, drag — and it flies differently. EPIC and above carry a sixth system on top of the four every pilot gets, bound to Q. Duplicates render down to scrap.
