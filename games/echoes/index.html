Two senses. Neither is safe.

THE LAMP — shows the room as it is. Colour, detail, what is alive. It burns the cell, and anything with eyes can see you holding it.

THE ECHO — a sonar ping paints the geometry in phosphor that fades over four seconds. It costs no power, only noise — and it is the loudest thing you can do. Phosphor is memory: it shows you where a wall was. It does not show what has moved since.

LUCIDITY — the dark eats it. Light and stillness give it back. At zero, the tape stops.
